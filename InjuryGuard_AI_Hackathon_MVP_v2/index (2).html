Simple guidance

Look after your body.

Small habits can help you notice problems earlier.

Pain
Notice when pain starts, what movement triggers it and whether it settles with rest. Persistent or worsening pain deserves professional attention.
Recovery
Sleep, rest days, hydration and sensible training progression all affect how ready your body feels.
Movement
If a familiar movement suddenly feels unstable, uneven or uncomfortable, reduce the load and reassess.
Return to play
After an injury, follow guidance from a qualified clinician or physiotherapist before demanding activity.
