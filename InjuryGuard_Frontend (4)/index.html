      SAFETY GUIDANCE

      Training and recovery guidance

      General safety information—not a diagnosis or treatment plan.

      01

      Before activity

      • Warm up gradually.
      • Check how your body feels before increasing intensity.
      • Use appropriate protective equipment.
      02

      During activity

      • Do not push through significant or worsening pain.
      • Pause if you feel unsafe or unable to continue normally.
      • Follow your coach's safety instructions.
      03

      After activity

      • Allow adequate rest and recovery.
      • Track changes in pain or function.
      • Follow your normal recovery routine.
      04

      When to get professional help

      • Symptoms are severe, worsening or persistent.
      • You cannot use the affected body part normally.
      • You are concerned about an injury or your safety.
